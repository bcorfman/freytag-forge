## Verdict

**The factory does not yet meet either goal.** Its saved baseline misses the intended causes, its structural repair route waits for the owner, and its measurements cannot yet certify a complete game. The next work is to close those specific gaps, then qualify diagnosis, repair and continuous play separately. A correct diagnosis, a safe refusal, a green scripted journey and a good isolated scene are different results; none alone is delivery.

### What must be true to claim success

**Unseen repair:** a frozen factory receives symptoms and a pinned source/evidence export, investigates without private answers, chooses the cause and repair itself, implements the repair, and passes independent behavioral and fun checks. It must also leave controls unchanged and honestly block missing evidence. The six-case, two-trial requirement remains: four defects, at least three causal mechanisms and two scenes, plus two controls. At least one previously unseen structural repair must finish through the persisted factory workflow. Require a later fresh live defect to transfer this result beyond the small sealed set. This is bounded demonstrated generalization, not a claim to solve every future defect.

**Whole game:** every preregistered final replicate starts a new ordinary 1A session and carries its actual facts through 1B, 1C, 2A, 2B, 2C, 3A and 3B to 3C. Each of the eight scene transitions is earned; 3C reaches the authored terminal ending through earned prerequisites. A costed recovery counts only with its cost committed and shown. No seeding, forced/timed handoff, skipped scene, unknown exit, fabricated progress or replacement of a failed replicate may count. Every scene has zero confirmed stuck turns and zero unresolved measurement cases; existing rejection/game-break gates remain. Complete prose, continuity and fun still pass. The older 80% experimental adoption bar cannot substitute for this all-replicate delivery bar.

A meaningful world response or exploration can be successful without a reveal. A pressure event that would occur anyway is not evidence that the player's action succeeded. Classify action outcome, knowledge progress, authored recovery, scene exit, rejected turn and unknown evidence separately. Use committed facts and action attribution, with a deterministic null-action comparison where needed; do not award progress merely because prose names an object. Freeze examples and counterexamples under the player-input guide before modifying either measurement path. A calibrated independent review handles semantic uncertainty; unresolved cases block certification. This proposes a measurement contract, not new fun-judge wording.

**Order.** Items 1 and 2 are free and belong in Step 2's task list as 2.7 and inside 2.1's fixtures. Items 3 to 7 are Opus design tasks; each is one capped session with a written estimate before it runs and an actual after, and none starts without the owner's word on the figures. N-3 is built through Step 4 and cannot start until the structural route exists; the unreachable-content gate (Step 6) can be built first and will fail on N-3 until it lands, which is its point.
