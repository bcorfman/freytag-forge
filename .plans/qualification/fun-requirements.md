**Fun requirements, in the order they bind.** Each "Opus designs" item means Opus owns the design and the check of the logic; I do not write fun-judge wording or thresholds in an orchestration prompt (existing rule). Designs are frozen and hashed before use.
1. **Step 2 measurement carries pacing numbers (free, not model-based).** Per scene and per run, computed from the four turn classifications and stored beside them: longest quiet stretch (turns without a reveal, pressure event or NPC move, counted after the first pressure event), turns to the first pressure event, scene length in turns, share of exploration turns, and count of earned reveals. These are the factory plan's own targets (quiet stretch at most four; scene length 10 to 13 turns on average), currently stated but computed nowhere. Reported, never silently gated, until Opus fixes the bounds (item 3).
2. **Character-knowledge flag in `provenance_flags`.** A character citing or acting on something not yet narrated or learned. It builds on the existing rule that a speaker line needs a narrated prerequisite (memory: speaker-lines-need-narrated-prerequisite). It covers "nobody knows what they have not learned", currently unmeasured. The free part is a lookup against committed facts and narrated events; semantic cases go to the calibrated review.
3. **Pacing window and bounds designed from the scene's Freytag phase (Opus designs).** The "pacing window" that `confirmed_stuck` depends on is defined numerically, per scene, from its Freytag phase (shorter in the climax, longer in the exposition), the quiet-stretch bound and the scene-length range. Frozen with the contract version so it cannot be tuned loosely after a bad result.
4. **Anti-flattening guards on every repair and on the probe keep rule (Opus designs the guard set).** "Fewer stuck turns than control" can be met by making the game duller: broader matchers, a reveal on every action, hint beats everywhere. A candidate must also not reduce earned reveals, NPC moves or exploration turns, must not breach the quiet-stretch or scene-length bounds, and must not lose an authored payoff the control delivered. Reported next to the stuck counts. This is a change to station code and to the probe's keep rule, so it needs the owner's word like other station work, and `factory/config/**` stays frozen.
5. **Station 10 as a whole-scene baseline (Opus designs the mode).** Today it judges one fix diff. Run it once over each whole scene as it stands, with the scene's neighbourhood, to get a fun baseline and rank the scenes that most need enriching. Read-only and capped; the output is a ranked list, not a gate.
6. **Whole-game fun gate (Opus designs and checks).** Reads each complete 1A–3C transcript against the owner's three-part definition (Freytag pacing, quality characters, continuity in and between scenes) plus delight (a memorable detail, twist or payoff a player would repeat to a friend). It must fail closed, be calibrated on labelled examples with measured error rates, and pass on the lower bound, as the other rate-based gates do. Design questions for Opus: the unit of judgment (whole transcript, per scene, or per act), how a long transcript reaches the judge within a bounded context, **Owner decision (2026-11-23): no Jev second opinion; Opus is the sole judge of the whole-game fun gate.** This is a recorded exception to the plan's rule that producer and grader come from different model families, because Claude writes the prose and Opus judges it. Compensate inside the design, not with a second model: calibrate on labelled examples including prose Claude wrote, report error rates, pass on the lower bound, and have the design say what the judge is shown so it cannot grade its own wording by recognition. Required by Step 8 and fed into Step 2.6's delivery-report validator, which today checks completion only; until it exists, "continuity and fun still pass" in the whole-game bar cannot be evaluated and must not be claimed.
7. **Authored contracts for Continuity Initiative (Opus designs the contract set; Claude drafts story text from the plot).** The contract fields that carry the designed fun (feeling line, bond beats, setups and payoffs, character cards) exist only in `factory/contracts/template.json` (every value `TODO`) and a test fixture; no authored contract exists for this story. Station 0 is supposed to create them. Author them for the nine scenes before the fun gate runs, so the judge has something to check the play against. Bios that prove too thin to judge `characters_in_character` are expanded under the existing bio rules (own ticket, lasting traits only, `plot.md` stays ground truth, every prompt stays under the 6,500-token cap, old and new bio listed in the PR).
