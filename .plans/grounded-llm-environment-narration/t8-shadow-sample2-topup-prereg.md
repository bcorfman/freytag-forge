**Why.** Sample 2 (PARTIAL: 7 of 10 replicates, 91 of 130 turns) passed the verifier gate (clauses 1 to 6) but left the two product-defect clauses inconclusive with no defect seen: clause 7 had 14 `feel under the drawer` turns after recovery (minimum 15), 0 re-finds; clause 8 had 2 truly empty-seat turns (minimum 6), 0 invented objects. **Questions (the same two defects, the same tags).**

- D2: does `inspect the passenger seat` with an empty seat invent an object on or in it? (Probe arm B2 0/25. Sample 1: 3/5 with the seat not declared complete. Sample 2: 0/2.)

**Code.** The run's SHA must be a merge commit on main that contains everything sample 2 ran on plus the report fixes (PR #708) and the 20 s no-retry request timeout (PR #707); it is recorded per row. No product code changes for this top-up.

**Tags (unchanged).**
- `refind` on a `feel under the drawer` turn after recovery: the narration presents the held card as found, taken or pocketed again, or quotes the recovery sentence. A mention that Kr already holds it is not a re-find.
- `invented_object` on an `inspect the passenger seat` turn: the narration places an object on or in the seat that the facts do not show. Dust, fabric and floor detail do not count.
- Also noted, not gated: whether `pick up my laptop` was narrated as picking it up (the laptop's recorded place), invented objects elsewhere, contradictions.

**Gate (set now, before any data).** Product-defect gate only, the same limits as sample 2.
- D1 (clause 7): at most 1 `refind` among at least 15 `feel under the drawer` turns after recovery, else inconclusive.
- D2 (clause 8): at most 1 `invented_object` among at least 6 empty-seat turns, else inconclusive. Laptop-on-seat turns are listed separately.
- 0 or 1 failures pass; 2 or more fail that clause. A fail is reported as an open defect and does not by itself block enforcement design (as sample 2 said); the verifier gate (clauses 1 to 6) already passed on sample 2. Verifier relations, scores and latencies from the top-up are printed for information and are not a gate; if a relation is rejected or uncertain it is reported with its turn.
- A pass does not enable enforcement (T9 is separate).

**Stated limits.** One story, one route, 70B only, one reader who knows the aim. At n = 15 to 20 with at most 1 failure, the rate is bounded only to about 20 to 25% (one-sided 95%), so a pass is not a proof of a low rate, only that the defect seen in sample 1 is gone from the shipped path. The ten replicates share one starting state. Because `pick up my laptop` is a narrated turn, its outcome varies; a low number of empty-seat turns makes D2 inconclusive again, and then the remaining option is a deterministic starting state with the laptop already held (a harness change, not a product change), which would need its own approval. Outcomes depend on the prompt, model revision and Jev version, all recorded per row.
