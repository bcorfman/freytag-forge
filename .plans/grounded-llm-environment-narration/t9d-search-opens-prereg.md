## Why

T9c stage F failed N1 and N2 by four turns, all `search the drawer` on route C. On 15 of 15 such turns the narrator wrote `"state":"open"` for the drawer in `item_facts` itself; 11 then showed the four supplies and committed, 4 omitted them in both attempts and the turn failed (rollback held). Where the engine opened the drawer (4 `look inside` turns where it was still closed) all 15 `look inside` turns showed the contents. So the failure is the narrator, not the engine, opening on `search`. A player who searches a drawer expects to see inside it, and the narrator already treats `search` that way, so the engine owns that step too. The gap nudge still repeats on `search` (the delivered text is appended either way; 15 of 15 `look inside` turns, kind `open`, restated it in T9c).

## The change (fixed now)

**1. `opens_container` wording (code, Ringer).** Searching the container now counts as opening it. In `storygame/runtime/jev_questions.py`:
- instructions: `Decide whether the command has the player open the container. Opening it, pulling it open or out, searching it, or going inside, in, into, or through it counts.`
- true: `The command opens the container, pulls it open, pulls it out, searches it, or says inside, in, into, or through the container. The player looks, feels, or reaches there.`
- false: `The command does not open the container, does not search it, and does not say inside, in, into, or through. A command that only looks at, inspects, examines, checks, closes, locks, touches, moves, carries, or talks about the container, or goes behind or on top of it, is false.`
Name, signature, return type and the `noul` type are unchanged. `searches_underneath` is unchanged (it passed T9b probe 2 and T9c stage P).

**2. The underside question is asked first (code, Ringer; story-agnostic).** In `WorldCapture._set_observation_request`, for a container or supporter focus the engine asks `searches_underneath` first. If it is true the kind is `under` and `opens_container` is not asked. Otherwise a container focus is asked `opens_container` (true gives kind `open`, as today) and anything else is kind `look`. Reason: `search under the drawer` now contains the word `search`, which `opens_container` counts, so the order must make the under word decide. The wording of both questions, the openable/closed/`sees` conditions of the engine open step and the rollback are unchanged. No names, verbs or story strings enter engine code.

**3. Nothing else in the engine, the story data or the matcher changes.** The nudge entry `k_sl_1a_f_r2` keeps its evidence (`search` stays in it), so `search the drawer` opens the drawer and also restates the gap.
