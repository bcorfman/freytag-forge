## Background you need

- A small, non-reasoning Llama-class narrator writes prose. The runtime, not the narrator, decides when a player has earned an authored reveal (`k_sl_*` items in `data/stories/continuity-initiative/knowledge.yaml`). When it does, authored text can reach the player: a reveal's `delivery_text`, a cue (`handoffs.yaml`), a forced or timer handoff, or the ending (storylet-route text). That text is written by the author and inserted by the runtime. Facts are the only mutable world truth. See `docs/PRD.md` and `docs/contributor-guide.md`.
- A hosted test, `@llm-canon` (`frontend/e2e/scene-runtime.spec.js`, prompts in `frontend/e2e/canon-journey.js`), plays a fixed script through all nine scenes on the staging server. A scene judge (`frontend/e2e/roleplay-judge.js`, `judgeSceneNarration`, model gpt-5.6-luna) then gives each scene seven booleans: `protected_safe`, `exit_motivated`, `rewards_investigation`, `scene_local`, `progressive`, `canon_consistent`, `rich`. That is 63 points per run. The runbook (`docs/testing-runbook.md` section 7) says within-run and between-run noise is as large as one- or two-point gaps, and that comparisons need several runs per arm.
- Each turn also has a typed attempt bundle (`storygame/runtime/attempt_bundle.py`): `outcome`, `transition_cause` (none, earned, forced, timer), `story_complete`, `selected_reveal.knowledge_ids` and `.delivery_fact_ids`, `cue_stage`, `committed_delta`.
- Project rules that bind any recommendation: `AGENTS.md`, `docs/narrator-writing-guide.md`, `docs/scene-fixing-guide.md`, `docs/world-model-grounding.md`. Prefer subtractive fixes over new narrator rules. No regex. No scene-specific runtime code. Recommend only techniques with prior evidence in the plan or guides; say plainly when you are proposing something new.
