# Story 1373

**Genre:** Adventure / Conspiracy Thriller

## Premise

The sudden disappearance of millions of people across the United States throws Jeremiah Thomas’s life into turmoil when his wife, investigative journalist Sarah Thomas, vanishes. His search leads him through a fractured, post-disappearance America and into a conspiracy involving government officials, private corporations, secret detention facilities, and a plan to reshape the country through fear and controlled reconstruction.

## Overall Setting

The story takes place in a near-future, post-disappearance United States. Major cities remain inhabited but unstable. Transportation networks are failing, emergency laws have replaced normal civil protections, and shortages have caused communities to become isolated and suspicious.

Los Angeles is under a federal emergency administration. Military checkpoints divide neighborhoods, abandoned vehicles remain on major roads, and official broadcasts repeatedly blame the disappearances on an unexplained national catastrophe.

Beyond public view, a network of government and corporate facilities operates beneath abandoned industrial sites, military installations, and emergency-management centers.

## Principal Characters

### Jeremiah James Thomas

A 33-year-old former structural engineer who initially wants only to find his wife. He is practical, persistent, and reluctant to trust conspiracy theories. His knowledge of infrastructure eventually makes him essential to infiltrating the conspirators’ underground facilities.

### Sarah Thomas

A 31-year-old investigative journalist whose research into secret emergency contracts places her at the center of the conspiracy. Although initially treated as someone Jeremiah must rescue, she possesses crucial information and has continued investigating from inside captivity.

### Gabriel Dexter

A 45-year-old former CIA operations officer who now lives as an isolated conspiracy researcher. His warnings about a shadow emergency program were widely dismissed before the disappearances. He helps Jeremiah but conceals his own past involvement in the program.

### Charles Jenkins

A 58-year-old former senior government official and the principal architect of the conspiracy. Charles believes democracy has become incapable of responding to national crises. He intends to replace it with a centralized system controlled by selected government and corporate leaders.

### Rebecca Jenkins

Charles’s 56-year-old wife and the chief executive of a biotechnology and defense contractor. She oversees the technical operation that made the disappearances possible. Unlike Charles, she is motivated less by ideology than by survival, influence, and the opportunity to control the rebuilding of the country.

**Setting:** Jeremiah and Sarah’s home in Los Angeles

**Characters:**

* Jeremiah James Thomas
* Sarah Thomas

**Plot:** Jeremiah returns home shortly after the mass disappearance and discovers that Sarah is missing. Evidence inside the house suggests that she was taken rather than simply vanishing with the others.

### Scene 1A.1 — The Empty House

Jeremiah reaches his neighborhood after navigating abandoned vehicles, stalled public transportation, and frightened crowds. Sarah is gone, but several details seem inconsistent with a sudden supernatural or unexplained disappearance:

* Her phone remains on the kitchen floor.
* Her laptop and work bag are missing.
* A chair has been overturned.
* The back door shows signs of forced entry.
* A small smear of blood appears beside the doorframe.

Jeremiah initially assumes looters entered the house after Sarah vanished, but the blood and missing work materials suggest that she may have still been present when someone arrived.

### Scene 1A.2 — Sarah’s Last Investigation

Jeremiah finds a hidden memory card taped beneath a drawer. It contains fragments of Sarah’s research into a federal emergency program called the **Continuity Initiative**.

The files show that private contractors constructed dozens of enormous “population stabilization centers” before the disappearances. Officially, the centers were designed as shelters for natural disasters and biological attacks.

Sarah’s notes contain one alarming sentence:

> They are not preparing to protect people. They are preparing to choose who remains.

### Scene 1A.3 — The Interrupted Message

Jeremiah recovers a damaged voice recording Sarah attempted to send shortly before she disappeared. In it, she says that government officials have been tracking her and that a planned “activation event” is imminent.

Before naming her source, Sarah hears someone enter the house. The recording ends after she whispers:

> Jeremiah, do not trust the emergency broadcasts.

This becomes the story’s **inciting incident**. Jeremiah realizes Sarah’s disappearance may be connected to the national catastrophe.

### Scene 1A.4 — The First Threat

A federal emergency patrol arrives at Jeremiah’s house unusually quickly. The officers claim they are conducting welfare checks, but they search Sarah’s office and ask specifically about her reporting.

Jeremiah conceals the memory card and pretends to know nothing. After the patrol leaves, he notices that one officer has quietly marked his front gate with a strip of reflective tape.

He understands that the authorities intend to return.

**Setting:** Jeremiah’s neighborhood and a damaged public park in Los Angeles

**Characters:**

* Jeremiah James Thomas
* Gabriel Dexter
* Sarah Thomas, through recordings and evidence

**Plot:** Jeremiah follows clues left by Sarah and discovers that the disappearances were coordinated through disguised emergency operations.

### Scene 1B.1 — Sarah’s Dead Drop

Sarah’s files reference an ordinary park bench where she exchanged information with a confidential source. Jeremiah travels there while avoiding checkpoints and emergency patrols.

Beneath the bench, he finds:

* A transit access card
* A handwritten sequence of numbers
* A photograph of Sarah speaking with an unidentified man
* A list of dates corresponding to earlier, smaller disappearances that had been reported as accidents or missing-person cases

The records suggest that the mass disappearance was preceded by years of secret tests.

### Scene 1B.2 — The Man Following Him

Jeremiah notices a man watching him from across the park. Believing the man works for the government, Jeremiah attempts to escape through abandoned service tunnels beneath the park.

The man follows but saves Jeremiah when an emergency patrol corners him. He identifies himself as Gabriel Dexter, the person in Sarah’s photograph.

Gabriel says Sarah contacted him because he had once worked on the Continuity Initiative.

### Scene 1B.3 — Gabriel’s Warning

Gabriel explains that the disappearances were not instantaneous. During the hours before the public became aware of the event, selected people were:

* Drugged through contaminated emergency water supplies
* Removed by disguised response teams
* Transported through evacuation tunnels
* Registered as missing before local authorities understood what had occurred

The scale was hidden by communications outages, manufactured panic, and falsified casualty data.

Gabriel claims the missing are still alive, but he refuses to explain how he knows.

### Scene 1B.4 — The Park Ambush

A tactical team arrives, proving Jeremiah was tracked from his house. Jeremiah and Gabriel escape through a storm-drain system, but Gabriel is forced to use specialized codes to unlock a secured maintenance gate.

Jeremiah realizes Gabriel retains access to government systems and may be more deeply involved than he admits.

**Plot:** Jeremiah and Gabriel locate one of the secret facilities and discover that it is only one part of a nationwide network.

### Scene 1C.1 — Following the Transport Route

The transit card from Sarah’s dead drop grants access to a supposedly abandoned freight terminal. Jeremiah recognizes that recent structural modifications conceal a large underground complex.

Fresh tire tracks, air vents, and unusually heavy electrical service confirm that the site remains active.

Jeremiah and Gabriel enter a service level but cannot reach the main facility without triggering security.

### Scene 1C.2 — Proof of the Captives

From an observation shaft, Jeremiah sees rows of sedated prisoners being moved through a processing area. Their identification numbers correspond to missing-person reports stored in Sarah’s files.

He briefly sees a woman resembling Sarah among a group being transferred, but the view is obscured before he can confirm her identity.

Jeremiah wants to enter immediately. Gabriel stops him, arguing that a reckless rescue attempt would cause the prisoners to be relocated or killed.

### Scene 1C.3 — The Nationwide Network

Gabriel accesses a logistics terminal and discovers that the Los Angeles site is not where all the missing people are held. It is a regional command center connected to facilities throughout the country.

The missing have been divided into categories:

* Political and military personnel
* Scientists, engineers, and medical specialists
* Journalists and public figures
* People labeled as potential resistance organizers
* Civilians selected for experimental programs
* Families of strategically valuable individuals

The conspiracy did not simply remove random citizens. It removed people who might either help control the country or resist that control.

### Scene 1C.4 — The Architects Revealed

A recorded conference shows Charles and Rebecca Jenkins discussing the next phase of the Continuity Initiative.

Charles intends to announce that the federal government has collapsed and establish a new emergency authority. Rebecca warns him that several facilities are overcrowded and that prisoners are beginning to resist.

Charles responds that the public will accept any government that promises to restore the missing.

Jeremiah now understands the central strategy: the conspirators created the catastrophe so they could later present themselves as the only people capable of ending it.

**Characters:**

* Jeremiah James Thomas
* Gabriel Dexter

**Plot:** Gabriel uses old intelligence contacts to create identities that will allow him and Jeremiah to enter the facility as technical inspectors.

### Scene 2A.1 — Gabriel’s Hideout

Gabriel takes Jeremiah to a fortified hideout inside an abandoned communications center. It contains surveillance equipment, weapons, emergency supplies, and years of files on the Continuity Initiative.

Jeremiah discovers that Gabriel has been preparing for the disappearance event for a long time.

Gabriel explains that he tried to expose the program but was discredited, dismissed, and labeled unstable.

### Scene 2A.2 — The Infiltration Plan

Jeremiah identifies a flaw in the facility’s cooling and structural-monitoring systems. Because the underground installation was expanded too quickly, parts of it require constant inspection.

Gabriel creates false credentials presenting them as specialists sent to investigate a dangerous support-column failure.

Their objectives are:

1. Enter without raising an alarm.
2. Copy the command center’s records.
3. Locate Sarah and the other captives.
4. Discover how prisoners are being transported.
5. Transmit the evidence to independent news and emergency networks.
6. Escape before Charles orders the facility purged.

### Scene 2A.3 — Entering the Facility

Jeremiah and Gabriel pass through several layers of security. Their identities survive the initial checks, but a supervisor questions why their inspection was not scheduled.

Jeremiah improvises, warning that the facility could suffer a progressive underground collapse. His technical explanation is convincing enough that the supervisor reluctantly permits them to continue.

This gives Jeremiah access to restricted infrastructure corridors that bypass the main security checkpoints.

### Scene 2A.4 — The First Complication

A facial-recognition system identifies Gabriel as a former Continuity Initiative officer. Instead of triggering a general alarm, the system quietly alerts Rebecca Jenkins.

Rebecca orders security to observe Gabriel rather than arrest him. She wants to know why he has returned and whom he has brought with him.

Jeremiah and Gabriel remain unaware that their infiltration has already been compromised.

**Plot:** Jeremiah and Gabriel uncover the true purpose of the experiments. Jeremiah also learns that Gabriel helped design the system used to select the missing.

### Scene 2B.1 — The Selection Algorithm

Inside the records archive, Jeremiah discovers files describing an artificial-intelligence system called **JANUS**.

JANUS analyzed government, financial, medical, employment, education, and communications data to classify every American according to:

* Usefulness to national reconstruction
* Likelihood of organized resistance
* Political influence
* Psychological vulnerability
* Family and social connections
* Potential value as leverage over others

Sarah was not taken merely because she discovered the conspiracy. JANUS predicted that her reporting could unite opposition groups after the disappearance.

Jeremiah was deliberately left behind because the system predicted he would lead investigators to Gabriel.

### Scene 2B.2 — Jeremiah Was Bait

The revelation transforms Jeremiah’s understanding of his journey. His discovery of the facility was not entirely accidental.

Charles expected Jeremiah to seek Gabriel. Security allowed portions of Sarah’s evidence to remain accessible so Jeremiah would expose Gabriel’s hidden network.

Jeremiah realizes that every step he has taken may have been anticipated.

### Scene 2B.3 — Gabriel’s Role

Jeremiah finds Gabriel’s name in the original JANUS development records. Gabriel admits that he helped design the system as a tool for identifying people at risk during national emergencies.

When he discovered that Charles intended to use it for mass detention and political control, he attempted to destroy the project. He failed and fled.

Sarah knew about Gabriel’s involvement but believed his access was the only way to expose the program.

Jeremiah feels betrayed. He suspects Gabriel may still be manipulating him to erase evidence of his own guilt.

### Scene 2B.4 — Sarah’s Hidden Resistance

The records reveal unusual equipment failures and corrupted prisoner files throughout the facility. Jeremiah recognizes phrases in the corrupted data that Sarah used in her private notes.

Sarah has created a covert network among the prisoners. Using her access to a medical terminal, she has been:

* Altering prisoner classifications
* Delaying transfers
* Hiding vulnerable captives from experimental programs
* Sending coded messages through maintenance reports
* Preparing prisoners for an organized uprising

Sarah is not passively waiting to be rescued. She has already begun dismantling the facility from within.

### Scene 2C.1 — Rebecca’s Offer

Rebecca contacts Gabriel privately and offers him safe passage. She claims Charles has become unstable and intends to eliminate both prisoners and lower-level conspirators once the new government is established.

She asks Gabriel to help her remove Charles and take control of the Continuity Initiative.

In exchange, she promises to release Sarah.

Gabriel pretends to consider the offer so he can gain access to the executive level.

### Scene 2C.2 — The Purge Order

Charles discovers that Sarah’s resistance network has corrupted JANUS records. Fearing that evidence will escape, he orders a full transfer of the most valuable captives and the destruction of everyone else.

The purge will begin within hours.

At the same time, Charles prepares a national broadcast in which he will claim to have located survivors of the catastrophe. He plans to release a small number of carefully selected captives and use their return to legitimize his new emergency government.

### Scene 2C.3 — Evidence or Rescue

Jeremiah and Gabriel obtain enough evidence to expose the conspiracy, but transmitting it will reveal their position and seal the detention sectors.

Gabriel argues that they must send the evidence immediately, even if doing so makes a rescue impossible. Without proof, freeing one group of prisoners will not stop the national operation.

Jeremiah refuses to abandon Sarah and thousands of other captives.

Their disagreement creates the story’s central **crisis choice**:

* Escape with the evidence and expose the conspiracy
* Attempt a rescue and risk losing both the evidence and their lives

### Scene 2C.4 — Sarah Changes the Choice

Sarah sends a coded message through the maintenance network. She has discovered a way to use the facility’s emergency broadcast system to transmit the evidence while simultaneously opening the detention sectors.

However, the broadcast system can only be activated manually from Rebecca’s secured office.

The apparent choice between exposure and rescue becomes a far more dangerous combined mission.

* Jeremiah James Thomas

**Plot:** Jeremiah and Gabriel descend into the detention levels while Sarah organizes the prisoners for an uprising.

### Scene 3A.1 — The Detention Block

Jeremiah enters the detention sector expecting rows of helpless captives. Instead, he finds Sarah coordinating prisoners through stolen radios, coded announcements, and sympathetic facility workers.

The reunion between Jeremiah and Sarah is brief because the purge countdown has begun.

Sarah explains that the facility contains only a fraction of the missing millions. Freeing them will matter only if the broadcast reveals the locations of the remaining sites.

### Scene 3A.2 — The Experiments

Sarah leads Jeremiah through a medical level where prisoners have been subjected to neurological and behavioral experiments.

Rebecca’s company has been testing methods for:

* Erasing short-term memory
* Increasing compliance through targeted stimulation
* Creating convincing false recollections
* Conditioning released captives to support official explanations
* Predicting resistance before it occurs

The people Charles intends to “rescue” during his broadcast have already been conditioned to endorse his version of events.

### Scene 3A.3 — The Unexpected Prisoner

Among the captives is a senior official publicly blamed for causing the catastrophe. He reveals that Charles imprisoned members of his own government and fabricated evidence against them.

He possesses authorization codes capable of overriding emergency military orders, but the codes will expire once Charles’s new authority is formally activated.

The rescue now has a strict deadline tied to Charles’s broadcast.

### Scene 3A.4 — The Uprising Begins

Sarah triggers coordinated disturbances across the detention sectors. Prisoners disable cameras, overwhelm isolated guards, and seize control of several internal checkpoints.

The uprising succeeds initially, but Charles seals the primary exits and redirects armed response teams toward the detention levels.

Jeremiah, Sarah, and Gabriel must fight upward toward Rebecca’s office while the prisoners hold the lower levels.

Charles appears remotely and locks down Rebecca’s office. He reveals that he has already transferred control of the national network away from her.

The broadcast system cannot operate while Charles controls the external communications relay. Gabriel reaches the relay chamber and manually disconnects it from JANUS.

The story closes with Charles observing the growing unrest from an unknown location. Although his conspiracy has been exposed, he believes the country is still moving toward the collapse he intended—and that frightened people may yet ask him to restore order.

# Freytag Structure Summary

## Exposition

Jeremiah and Sarah’s relationship, the post-disappearance crisis, Sarah’s investigation, and the unstable condition of Los Angeles are established.

## Inciting Incident

Jeremiah discovers evidence that Sarah was abducted and that her disappearance is connected to the Continuity Initiative.

## Rising Action

Jeremiah meets Gabriel, discovers the secret facility, learns the missing are alive, infiltrates the installation, uncovers JANUS, and discovers both Gabriel’s guilt and Sarah’s resistance network.

## Crisis

Jeremiah must seemingly choose between transmitting evidence and rescuing the captives. Sarah reveals a dangerous plan capable of accomplishing both.

## Climax

Jeremiah, Sarah, and Gabriel seize the emergency broadcast system while the prisoners revolt. Gabriel holds open the communications relay, Sarah exposes the conspiracy nationally, and Jeremiah defeats the facility’s predictive security system.

## Falling Action

The captives escape, Rebecca is captured, the facility collapses, and detention centers across the country begin responding to the broadcast.

## Resolution

Jeremiah and Sarah reunite and commit themselves to freeing the remaining captives. Charles escapes, while the discovery of “Phase Two” creates a larger unresolved threat.
